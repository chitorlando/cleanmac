CleanMac 0.1.1

  • A welcome screen on first launch to grant Full Disk Access and Finder automation. You can return to it from CleanMac › Permissions….
  • Permissions now survive updates: releases are signed with a stable certificate of their own. Coming from 0.1.0, you'll grant them one last time.
  • Clear messages when macOS protects an item, such as another app's container, with buttons to show it in Finder or open the Full Disk Access settings.
  • The Uninstaller marks leftovers that macOS protects before moving them.

Free and open source. Distributed without Apple notarization.
